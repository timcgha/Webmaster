# WM-003 implementation design (candidate, not independent QA acceptance)

The compact south training extension uses the existing fixed-step swing/collision solver, an explicit surface/pull state module, and authored geometry. It does not replace the engine or legacy skyline. The entry gap is twelve units wide, with a real wrist-web swing. A striped wall requires contact, climb held, facing within60 degrees, and no intervening solid. Attachment range is1.15 units; body radius/height remain0.48/3.4. Wall speed3.4 and ceiling speed3.5 units/second are difficulty independent. The wall at z=-61 has one ceiling junction at y=11, x=-10..-3; only this authored wall/ceiling pair transitions. Transition blends over0.24 seconds. Wall controls are up/down and camera-consistent lateral movement; on the ceiling the same camera-relative ground plane is retained, so S/back moves away from the wall. The surface camera keeps an upright horizon, blends toward beta1.52, and bounds orbit to the visible wall hemisphere. Ordinary/WM002 camera code paths are retained.

Climb is C/RB/R1 and pull Q/LB/L1. Existing E/LT/L2 swing, run, menu, recenter, jump and diagnostic semantics remain. RB/LB are now relevant standard-mapping controls for activity/neutral detection; the prior unused-button test moves its fixture from newly mapped RB to still-unused index8, retaining the assertion. Standard mapping only; synthetic devices are not physical proof.

Pull chooses the best eligible visible aim candidate deterministically (dot score, then range, then stable ID). If none is eligible it reports the best visible failure cause, or obstruction. Aim cone36 degrees; LIGHT range15/speed3.4, LIMITED range7/speed1.3, HEAVY immovable. Acceleration9 units/s²; minimum hero/object center separation1.3 plus object half-width (2.8 for the three-unit-wide light step). No throwing/impact/combat. Pullable objects are authored floor-supported training crates with deterministic reset positions. One wrist web only, target and visible selected outline remain bound to the active pull until release, and every release/obstruction/range/lifecycle boundary clears force. Turning to the aim direction occurs before computing wrist contact, preventing a backwards-facing hand from entering the finish ledge.

Route proof requires entry ring attachment and deliberate airborne release plus far-roof landing, wall vertical progress>=4 and lateral>=3, ceiling travel>=6 and a grounded drop, designated crate displacement>=5 to x<-6.3, standing on the moved crate, and a grounded finish-ledge landing. Ledge top2.6 exceeds ordinary jump height; crate top1.3 makes two genuine jumps possible. The light step is three units wide and the upper landing platform extends west to x=-16, giving a forgiving landing without changing the access edge at x=-10 or the collision capsule. Teleport-sized steps invalidate progress. Finish fires once; no later-sprint unlock.

Save envelope/schema/storage keys stay v1. An optional climb:{version:1,checkpoint:0|1|4|6,completed:boolean} preserves supported WM001/WM002 records without migration. Checkpoint6 requires completed=true. Earlier checkpoint with completed=true represents an earned historical completion during replay, matching Skyline semantics; it never restores finish-stage placement or awards a new completion. Current replay stages still must be earned. Climb/ceiling/pull/swing/air/landing/recovery cannot be saved; dynamic crate-top standing is also not an approved save floor. Manual/checkpoint generations remain separate. Load/retry/fall restore an earned static safe checkpoint and deterministic crate arrangement, never arbitrary object velocities/targets/constraints/held input/surface or camera state. Pause freezes motion and clears traversal commands; resume detaches predictably and requires fresh input. Focus/controller loss releases pull and surface commands; fall recovery remains available.

Presentation is original procedural geometry and color: red#df4052, blue#2456bd with original orange chest emblem. White eyes moved to y3.12 and outward/upward45-degree long-axis angle. One original lattice line system per torso/waist/arm/leg region combines six meridians and five scalloped rings, no textures or copied artwork. Swing leg angles use signed actual velocity and rope phase, bounded±0.65 radians with exponential blend; no clock-driven kick oscillator and no collision/physics parameter change. The rendered pivot uses the opposite X sign to the pose signal because the downward leg points along local -Y; read-only world-space hip/tip displacement assertions independently verify actual trailing and forward geometry. Healthy far-roof landing and neutral leg return are required in the presentation journey. S3 entry swing status is offset below the long objective, and ordinary card rectangles are checked for viewport bounds and pairwise overlap at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 1194×834 during entry, wall, ceiling and pulling. No dependency, license, external artwork/audio or paid service changes.

All captures and passes are Implementer evidence; independent QA and sponsor appearance/comfort/physical-device judgment remain required. Parent baseline and exact historical-writer compatibility fixtures retain parent attribution.
